CRM automations that support speed, ownership, and visibility.

  • One source of truth: Keep contacts, conversations, deals, notes, files, and activity history in a structured CRM environment.
  • Consistent lead handling: Route leads, create tasks, send notifications, and trigger follow-ups based on clear business rules.

CRM automation and system workflow deliverables.

  • Lead capture and routing: Connect forms and lead sources, then assign records based on source, service, territory, or workload.
