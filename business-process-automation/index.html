What is the difference between workflow and business process automation?

Workflow automation usually focuses on a defined sequence of tasks. Business process automation can cover a broader operation involving several workflows, systems, teams, and reporting requirements.

Can we implement the project in phases?

Yes. A phased rollout can prioritize the highest-impact process, reduce risk, and create a foundation for additional automation.
